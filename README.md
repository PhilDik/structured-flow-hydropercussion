**Hydropercussive acoustic generation by rapid structural modulation of water flow**

Status: **early public concept note**  
This is not a patent filing, not a finished device, and not a claim of a working prototype.

## Concept

Structured Flow Hydropercussion is a concept for generating sound by rapidly changing the internal structure of a water flow.

The core idea is not to use water as a loudspeaker, and not to mechanically redirect a stream like a valve-based water instrument. Instead, the water flow itself is treated as a physical acoustic medium. Rapid structural modulation may cause changes in breakup behavior, droplet formation, turbulence, splashes, impacts, hissing, murmuring, or other hydropercussive acoustic events.

In short:

rapid structural modulation of water flow
→ change in droplets / breakup / turbulence / impact behavior
→ hydropercussive acoustic events
